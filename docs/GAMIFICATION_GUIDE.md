# 🏆 Student Gamification & Academic Karma Guide

This guide details the reputation points formula, tier progression, and unlockable achievement badges on the platform.

---

## 1. Academic Karma Formula

Students accumulate academic karma through meaningful community contributions:
- **Verified Note Upload**: `+20 Karma`
- **Material Receives 5-Star Rating**: `+5 Karma`
- **Peer Review Marked Helpful**: `+2 Karma`
- **Material Flagged as Spam/Rejected**: `-15 Karma`

---

## 2. Scholar Tier Progression

| Tier | Min Karma | Badge Icon | Privileges |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Novice Scholar** | 0 | ⚪ | Access all public materials |
| **Apprentice Scholar** | 50 | 🔵 | Highlighted profile, fast upload review |
| **Senior Contributor** | 150 | 🟢 | Peer review voting rights |
| **Distinguished Fellow** | 300 | 🟣 | Featured contributor on department homepage |
| **Academic Grandmaster** | 600 | 🟡 | Direct note publishing badge & community moderation |

---

## 3. Achievement Badges

- 🌱 **First Contributor**: Uploaded your first study resource.
- 📚 **Prolific Scholar**: Uploaded 5+ verified study materials.
- 🏛️ **Community Pillar**: Uploaded 10+ verified study materials.
- ⭐ **Master Educator**: Maintained average resource rating $\ge 4.5$.
- 💯 **Karma Centurion**: Reached 100+ academic karma.
- 👑 **Campus Legend**: Reached 500+ academic karma.
