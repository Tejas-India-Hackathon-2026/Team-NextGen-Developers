# 🏆 Tejas India Hackathon 2026: NextGen Campus Portal

## 🌟 The Problem
College students struggle with fragmented academic resources: syllabus notes scattered across WhatsApp/Telegram groups, missing previous year exam papers, attendance calculation stress, and lack of verified study guides.

---

## 💡 Our Solution: Student Resource Sharing Platform
An all-in-one digital academic ecosystem combining:
1. **Curated PDF Study Notes Hub**: Verified by faculty, searchable via inverted fuzzy index.
2. **PyPDF Security & Integrity Scanner**: Automatically blocks empty or malicious files.
3. **Academic Karma & Gamification**: Rewards contributors with badges and unlocks.
4. **Interactive Revision Tools**: Practice quizzes, flashcards, and exam countdown planner.
5. **Smart Bunk & Attendance Forecaster**: Predicts safe bunk allowances to keep students above 75%.
6. **Career & Placement Hub**: Internships, alumni mentors, and project ideas.

---

## 🚀 Key Innovations & Tech Highlights
- **Python + Streamlit**: Ultra-fast, clean reactive web application.
- **Pure Python Modular Engine**: Zero unnecessary heavy dependencies.
- **Security-First Architecture**: SHA-256 credentials, sliding-window rate limiting, and XSS sanitization.
